Senior Backend Engineer (Python)

About the role
We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to design and scale the APIs behind our
B2B analytics platform. You will own services end to end, mentor mid-level
engineers, and drive technical decisions across the backend team. This is a
hybrid role based in Berlin (3 days on-site).

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and operate RESTful and async APIs in Python.
- Model and optimize data in PostgreSQL; own schema and query performance.
- Containerize services with Docker and deploy on AWS (ECS/EKS).
- Build CI/CD pipelines and improve observability and reliability.
- Mentor engineers and lead code reviews and design discussions.

Requirements (mandatory)
- 5+ years of professional backend development experience.
- Strong Python and at least one modern web framework (FastAPI or Django).
- Solid experience with PostgreSQL and SQL performance tuning.
- Hands-on AWS experience and Docker in production.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
- Must be eligible to work in the EU.

Nice to have
- Kubernetes, Terraform, or other IaC tools.
- Experience with event-driven systems (Kafka, RabbitMQ).
- Prior experience mentoring or leading a small team.

We offer competitive salary, equity, learning budget, and flexible hours.
